# Piano miniatures

Seven original short scores and a deterministic synthesized piano voice were
created for this site at the user's request. No commercial recording, sample
pack, existing composition excerpt or external audio URL was used. These are
**digital piano miniatures, not recordings of Edoardo Amendola performing**.
They must not be credited as his performances.

The musical direction is quiet and lyrical, with open intervals, lightly
arpeggiated harmonies, flexible timing and decay. The website's Debussy and
Rachmaninoff repertoire informed the general mood, not a claim about favorites.
All seven pieces share D-major/B-minor colors to make transitions coherent.

| Movement | Duration | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silence | 7.9 s | A short opening pause, then two very soft tones |
| Listening | 9.0 s | An open bass and a four-note question |
| Breath | 8.9 s | A gently rising arpeggio with room between notes |
| Rubato | 10.25 s | Uneven phrase timing, briefly gathering then relaxing |
| Cantabile | 12.75 s | A small singing melody over sparse accompaniment |
| Resonance | 9.6 s | One spread, open chord and its long decay |
| Da Capo | 10.3 s | A quieter return of the Listening motif |

Files: stereo MP3, 44.1 kHz, 160 kb/s. Durations exclude minor codec padding.
Playback is opt-in, at reduced gain, and fades when the movement changes.
Scores and synthesis source: `../../tools/render-piano.py`.
